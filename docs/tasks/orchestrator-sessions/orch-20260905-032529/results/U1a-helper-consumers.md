Internal functions are not made public. No caller here gained a school argument in U1a, except the auth viewer queries documented in U1a.md. Existing default, explicit-school, owned-record, capability and storage checks remain at their original domain seams. Domain owners must inspect actual call paths before enabling switching; **none of this table is a scoped-route allowlist**. This captures the indirect blast radius without claiming universal enforcement or inventorying unrelated APIs.
