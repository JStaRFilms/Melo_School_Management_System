# Task D-01: Compliance Control Dossier (F5)

## Objective
Produce the versioned engineering/compliance basis required before broad sensitive-processing rollout (mailbox provisioning, AI imports, cross-branch/school transfers, analytics, notifications).

## Scope
- Data inventory and classification across all Melo subsystems: identity, contact, education, finance, attendance, health, safeguarding, disciplinary, biometric/media, authentication, audit, AI inputs/outputs, and provider metadata.
- Purpose/lawful-basis matrix and controller/processor/subprocessor boundaries for Melo, school tenants, third-party providers, and transfer participants.
- Guardian, child-age, parental consent, subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability), retention, breach response, and international transfer controls.
- Nigeria-first jurisdiction matrix (NDPA 2023, NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive, Child's Rights Act) expanded to secondary launch markets (GDPR/UK GDPR, COPPA/FERPA, POPIA, Kenya DPA, Ghana DPA) with dated primary citations.
- Traceability from compliance controls to product features: H5 (institutional email), F3 (AI imports), H8 (usage metering), H9 (asset library & quarantine), F4 (transfers), and audit/notifications.
- Explicit counsel questions and actionable feature release gates.

## Definition of Done
- Engineering research, not legal advice; explicitly states no claim of legal approval.
- Complete cited jurisdiction register with official source URLs, dates, and review schedules.
- Controls-to-product matrix covering all sensitive domains.
- Clear separation between hard legal requirements, best practices, and unresolved counsel questions.
- Market-specific launch gates defined.
