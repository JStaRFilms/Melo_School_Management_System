# Material-led hero proof

The miniature table studies were rejected for looking schematic and too close to vector illustration. This is a new image for review, not an approved replacement in the browser draft.

## Composition

A close-up of an imagined cyan work surface, folded uniform-inspired fabric, layered paper lettering spelling `olive`, and a fan of pigmented paper. The letters are draft illustration, not a redesign of the school's official logo. Nothing is represented as actual school furniture or pupil work.

The image uses a perspective camera, physical contact shadows, shallow depth of field, lacquer highlights, cotton sheen and a procedural warp/weft bump, paper grain, and visible laminated edges. There are no chairs, graduation caps, stock models, or school claims.

The first diagnostic image used a paper arch. It was replaced because it made the scene anonymous. `material-proof.png` preserves that early diagnostic; `material-hero.png` is the later lettering composition.

## Actual output

`material-hero.png` is a 1200 × 750 Cycles render using 24 samples, denoising, AgX medium-high contrast, and four CPU threads. The final render succeeded in 42.32 seconds. A 640-pixel diagnostic was rendered before the larger image.

`build_material_hero.py` creates only a separate scene. `render_material_hero.py` renders an already-created scene. Neither script saves or exports a Blender file. The modelling script uses a locally installed Arial Rounded font; it does not redistribute the font file.

The original scene and previous concepts remain intact. The original B page is unchanged. No animation, browser integration, new publication, or `.blend` export has been performed for this proof.

## Review point

Judge the composition and material treatment before increasing resolution or building motion. The textile motif is stylised, and the lettering treatment is exploratory. This is not a claim that the final art direction has met the user's quality bar.
